What it knows

One bar. Every kind of question.

Beam skips the "which app should I open" step. Type the thing, get the answer.

Money math

Numbers with dollar signs, dashes, and abbreviations — sane.

Currency-aware arithmetic that keeps the units straight, expands k/m/b, and handles calculator-style percentages.

$450,344.20 − 100k − 10815
$339,529.20
Historical FX

Any currency, any date, back to 1999.

ECB reference rates via Frankfurter (free, no API key). "on september 2025", "5/21", or a full date — Beam figures it out.

1 usd to cny on 5/21/25
7.20 CNY on 2025-05-21
Date arithmetic

Bare numbers default to days after a date.

Chain months, weeks, and days — or skip the unit word entirely when the base is obviously a date.

10/1 + 30 + 25
November 25, 2026
Calendar quick-add

Type an event the way you'd say it.

Title, time, and multi-part venue parsed together. Shift+Enter to pick a calendar; a default calendar lives in Settings.

Global Entry Interview JFK Airport Terminal 4 8:30 fri
Fri, Jul 31 · 8:30 AM at JFK Airport, Terminal 4
Contacts & calling

Ring a phone from your Mac, silently.

Enter dials via iPhone Continuity through Contacts.app — no popup, no FaceTime window. "All options…" pops a menu with every phone, email, and FaceTime target.

Fesco
FESCO Trading Co. +1 (212) 260-0666 · call, message, FaceTime
Places

Address lookup with a name attached.

Apple Maps under the hood, US-biased region. Grab a full-formatted address or drop the place straight into a calendar event.

mount sinai hospital nyc
The Mount Sinai Hospital 1468 Madison Ave, New York, NY 10029
Time zones

Convert time across cities or US states.

"12:30pm ny to utah", "3pm to sydney" — cities, states, or TZ identifiers all work.

12:30pm ny to utah
12:30 PM New York → 10:30 AM Denver
Units & unicode

Everything else — because sometimes you just need one.

Bare units auto-convert (58" → 147 cm, 5 lb → 2.27 kg). Emoji + Unicode grids with keyboard focus. Ask AI via Ollama.

147.32 cm
Setup

Two steps, then ⌥Space forever.

i.

Download and drag Beam.app to Applications.

Signed with Apple Developer ID and notarized. macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later. Universal binary — Apple Silicon and Intel.

ii.

Open once. Press ⌥ Space.

Grant Contacts, Calendar, and Automation the first time each is asked. Updates arrive automatically via Sparkle — no App Store required.
