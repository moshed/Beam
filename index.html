What it knows

One bar. Every kind of question.

Beam skips the "which app should I open" step. Type the thing, get the answer.

Money math

Numbers with dollar signs, dashes, and abbreviations — sane.

Currency-aware arithmetic that keeps units straight, expands k/m/b, and handles calculator-style percentages.

Labels stay in the copy

Type notes with the numbers. They stay for context, not calculation.

Words like "chickens", "lbs", "people" get struck through so the math runs clean — but Copy query + result keeps them.

Date arithmetic

Bare numbers default to days after a date.

Chain months, weeks, and days — or skip the unit entirely when the base is obviously a date.

Calendar quick-add

Type an event the way you'd say it.

Title, time, and location parsed together. Shift+Enter to pick a calendar; a default calendar lives in Settings.

Contacts & calling

Ring a phone from your Mac, silently.

Enter dials via iPhone Continuity through Contacts.app — no popup, no FaceTime window. "All options…" pops a menu with every phone, email, and FaceTime target.

Multi-section results

One query. App, definition, files, emoji — all grouped.

Beam merges results across every searcher and lets you reorder or rebind sections in Settings.

Emoji & unicode

A grid you can keyboard through.

Type any word, get every relevant emoji + unicode glyph. Press Down to focus the grid (blue), then Left/Right/Up/Down to pick.

Setup

Two steps, then ⌥Space forever.

i.

Download and drag Beam.app to Applications.

Signed with Apple Developer ID and notarized. macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later. Universal binary — Apple Silicon and Intel.

ii.

Open once. Press ⌥ Space.

Grant Contacts, Calendar, and Automation the first time each is asked. Updates arrive automatically via Sparkle — no App Store required.
